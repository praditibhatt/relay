Before we connect,
here's exactly what we read.

Your data is yours. Relay reads only what it needs to help you — and only with your permission.

What we read
Email metadata
Sender · recipient · timestamp · subject · thread structure
So we know who you're talking to and how often
Calendar events
Title · time · attendees · duration · recurrence
So we know when meetings happen and who attended
Deal tracker
Contact names · deal stages · custom fields
So your existing pipeline isn't empty on day one
What we never read
Financial data Passwords Personal files
How we use your data
Warmth scoring · follow-up drafts · write-back to your tracker · nothing else
We never sell, share, or train on your data
Your data is used only for Relay. Not for ads, not for third parties, not for model training.
You're always in control
Toggle any source off anytime. Request permanent deletion from Settings — processed within 24h.
Relay · Data Policy v1.0 · March 2026
What Relay reads
Email metadata — sender, recipient, timestamp, subject, thread structure.
Calendar events — title, time, attendees, duration.
Deal tracker — contact names, stages, custom fields.
Call log metadata — who you called, when, how long. No audio.
What we never read
Financial data · Passwords · Personal files · Email bodies · Attachments · BCC recipients · Deleted items · Meeting notes
How we use your data
Warmth scoring · follow-up drafts · write-back to your tracker. Nothing else.
Our commitments
✓ We never sell your data
✓ We never train AI on your data
✓ Toggle any source off anytime from Settings
✓ Permanent deletion on request — 24h turnaround
✓ Read-only access — Relay never writes without your approval
✓ Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
